The Tithe

Ten percent of profit, given without exception.

Every garment sold returns ten percent of its profit to pro-life charities — the ones that house expectant mothers, fund pregnancy care, and defend the unborn when defending them costs something.

It is not a campaign and there is no month for it. Ten percent of profit leaves this company every year, whether the year was good or bad, and we publish where it went.
